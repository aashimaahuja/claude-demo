About Me

I'm a Frontend Engineer at Meta with a deep passion for React, performance optimization, and building great user experiences. Beyond my day job, I love helping others in the tech community by creating courses, mentoring, and speaking at international conferences.

I believe in writing clean, maintainable code and sharing the lessons I learn along the way. Whether it's through a live workshop or an open-source contribution, my goal is to make frontend development more accessible to everyone.
